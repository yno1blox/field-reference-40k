## Extraction safeguards

Publication markers are selected separately from chapter/allegiance logos. Descendants belonging to another nested datasheet are excluded. Both standard and mixed-model characteristic wrappers are supported. Weapon and model-row counts are checked against each source card. All 1,655 source cards were represented, with no parser count warnings.

The 512 Legends flags are explicit source classifications, all with edition 11 markers. Each imported record has its own source evidence; the edition in a page URL is not treated as evidence for every card.

Keyword scopes and raw points-row labels are retained. Conditional invulnerable saves link back to the source for their conditions. Numerical strings are not reinterpreted. Equipment grouping for new units is not used to claim legal configurations.

## Remaining limitations

The source is user-approved Wahapedia, not an independent official PDF audit. Points publication dates are unknown. Rules prose, full equipment constraints, detachments, stratagems, enhancements and photos are absent. Source completeness is limited to the supplied pages. Shared units may appear in multiple faction catalogues.
